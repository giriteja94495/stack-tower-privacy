Stack Tower – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Stack Tower ("the App") is developed by Giriteja. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress on your device: which levels you have completed, your star ratings, your best scores, and your sound and haptics settings. This data stays on your device by default and is not sent anywhere unless you choose to use the optional online leaderboard.

Optional online leaderboard

If you choose to use the leaderboard, your device creates an anonymous account. No email, password, or personal information is required. The App uploads the following to our database:

  • A username you pick (or a randomly generated one). You can change it up to 3 times every 30 days.
  • Your total stars, the number of levels you have completed, and your best level score.

This data is visible to other players on the public leaderboard. You can stop using the leaderboard at any time by not playing while connected; your local progress is unaffected.

Network access

The App works fully offline for gameplay. It only connects to the internet when you use the optional online leaderboard.

Advertising and tracking

The App contains no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking. We do not sell or share data, because we don't have any.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
