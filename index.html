Stack Tower – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Stack Tower ("the App") is developed by Giriteja. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress on your device only: which levels you have completed, your star ratings, your best scores, and your sound and haptics settings. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the App removes it.

Network access

The App works fully offline. It does not send or receive data over the internet.

Advertising and tracking

The App contains no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking. We do not sell or share data, because we don't have any.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on this repository.
